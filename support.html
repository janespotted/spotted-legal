Spotted Support

Invite-only nightlife in New York City.

For help with your account, an invitation, or a safety concern, email jane@spotted.social. Include your username and what happened. Keep passwords and verification codes private.

Joining Spotted

Use the phone number on your invitation. During signup, you can type both nominations by hand if you prefer to keep Contacts access off.

Location sharing

You can turn sharing off in Spotted. You can also change Spotted’s location permission in iPhone Settings. Plans, Messages, and venue discovery work with location access off.

Report or block someone

Use the menu on a post or a person’s profile to report a concern. Choose Block on a person’s profile to stop them from seeing your posts or location.

Delete your account

Open Profile, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. Follow the confirmation shown in the app.
