Spotted Support

For help with your account, an invitation, or a safety concern, email jane@spotted.social. Include your username and what happened. Keep passwords and verification codes private.

Joining Spotted

If you received an invitation, use the phone number it was sent to. If Spotted asks you to nominate people, you can enter both phone numbers by hand with Contacts access off.

Location sharing

You can turn sharing off in Spotted. You can also change Spotted’s location permission in iPhone Settings. Plans, Messages, and venue discovery work with location access off.

Report or block someone

Open the person’s Friend ID card, tap More options, and choose Report user or Block. You can also report a post from its menu.

Delete your account

Open Profile, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. Type DELETE and confirm.
