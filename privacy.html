Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026
Last Updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spotted ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Profile content: Photos, videos, captions, comments, and other content you post or share through the Service.
  • Messages: The content of direct messages and group chats you send through the Service.
  • Contact information: If you grant permission, we access your device contacts to help you find friends who are also using the Service. We use phone numbers from your contacts to identify matches; we do not store your full contact list.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Location information: With your permission, we collect precise location data (GPS coordinates) to show you nearby venues, friends, and activity. Location data is essential to the core functionality of the Service.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, app version, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage information: How you interact with the Service, including features used, content viewed, and actions taken.
  • Log data: IP address, access times, and crash reports.

Information from Third Parties

  • If you sign in using Apple Sign In or Google Sign In, we receive basic profile information from those services consistent with the permissions you grant.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Send you SMS verification codes for phone number verification
  • Match you with friends already using the Service
  • Show you relevant venues, activity, and people based on your location
  • Enable communication between users
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Monitor for fraud, abuse, and policy violations
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. SMS Messaging

When you provide your phone number, we may send you SMS messages to verify your identity. These messages are sent only when you explicitly request verification (e.g., during sign-up or when adding a phone number to your profile). We do not send marketing or promotional SMS messages.

By providing your phone number and tapping "Send Code" or similar button in the App, you consent to receive automated SMS messages from Spotted. Standard message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. Reply HELP for assistance. We use Twilio as our SMS provider.

4. How We Share Your Information

With Other Users

  • Profile information you make visible (name, photo, etc.) is shown to other users as configured by your privacy settings
  • Posts, comments, and shared content are visible according to the audience you select
  • Your active status, location, and check-ins may be visible to your friends per your settings

With Service Providers

We share information with third parties that provide services on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase (database hosting and authentication)
  • Twilio (SMS delivery)
  • Apple and Google (sign-in services)
  • Mapping providers (location services)
  • Analytics and crash reporting providers

These providers are contractually required to handle your data only for the purposes we specify.

For Legal Reasons

We may share information when required by law, in response to legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Spotted, our users, or others.

Business Transfers

If Spotted is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Access and Deletion: You can access and edit your profile information within the App. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below or use the in-app deletion option in Settings.

Location Permissions: You can grant or revoke location permissions through your device settings. Note that some features of the Service will not work without location access.

Contacts Permission: You can grant or revoke contacts permission through your device settings. Without contacts access, we cannot help you find friends already on the Service.

Notifications: You can manage push notifications through your device settings and SMS messages by replying STOP.

California Residents (CCPA): If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your information, and not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

Other US States: Residents of certain other US states (including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah) may have similar rights. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Some content (such as posts, status, and check-ins) automatically expires per the design of the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we must retain it for legal, security, or business reasons.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 17, we will delete it.

9. International Users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed in the US. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.
